THE CONFLICT

What Was World War II?

World War II was a global conflict that lasted from September 1, 1939 to September 2, 1945. It arose from unresolved tensions of the First World War, the Great Depression, and the rise of fascism and totalitarianism across Europe and Asia.

The war pitted the Allied Powers — led by the United Kingdom, United States, Soviet Union, and France — against the Axis Powers — led by Nazi Germany, Fascist Italy, and Imperial Japan.

From the trenches of North Africa to the frozen steppes of Russia, from the islands of the Pacific to the beaches of Normandy — this war touched every corner of the earth and forever changed the course of human history.

YEAR BY YEAR

Timeline of the War

1939

The War Begins

Nazi Germany invades Poland on September 1. Britain and France declare war on Germany two days later. The Soviet Union invades Poland from the east under the Molotov–Ribbentrop Pact.

1940

Fall of France & Battle of Britain

Germany rapidly conquers Denmark, Norway, Belgium, Netherlands, and France. The Dunkirk evacuation saves 300,000 Allied troops. The Luftwaffe launches massive air raids on Britain — and the RAF holds the line.

1941

A World at War

Germany launches Operation Barbarossa — the largest land invasion in history — against the Soviet Union. Japan attacks Pearl Harbor on December 7, bringing the United States into the war. The conflict truly becomes global.

1942

The Turning Point

The Battle of Midway cripples Japan's naval power. In North Africa, the Allies begin pushing back Rommel. The brutal Battle of Stalingrad begins — a six-month siege that will become the deadliest battle in history.

1943

The Tide Turns

Germany surrenders at Stalingrad — a catastrophic blow. The Allies invade Sicily and Italy. German forces are defeated in North Africa. In the Pacific, the island-hopping campaign intensifies.

1944

D-Day & Liberation

Operation Overlord — the largest seaborne invasion in history — lands 156,000 Allied troops on the beaches of Normandy on June 6. Paris is liberated. Soviet forces push westward. Germany launches its last desperate Ardennes offensive.

1945

Victory & New World Order

Soviet forces reach Berlin. Adolf Hitler dies on April 30. Germany surrenders unconditionally on May 8 (V-E Day). Atomic bombs are dropped on Hiroshima and Nagasaki. Japan surrenders on September 2 — the war is over.

AFTERMATH

The War's Lasting Legacy

World War II permanently reshaped the political, social, and technological landscape of our world.

🌐

United Nations

Founded in 1945 to prevent future global conflicts, the UN established an international framework for diplomacy, peacekeeping, and human rights.

☢️

Nuclear Age

The atomic bombings of Hiroshima and Nagasaki ushered in the nuclear age, defining Cold War geopolitics for decades and threatening mutually assured destruction.

🔬

Technological Revolution

Radar, jet engines, computers, penicillin, and rocketry — technologies born of wartime necessity transformed post-war civilian life and the modern world.

📜

Human Rights

The Holocaust galvanized the world to adopt the Universal Declaration of Human Rights in 1948, establishing fundamental rights for all of humanity.

🌍

Decolonization

The weakening of European empires accelerated independence movements across Asia, Africa, and the Middle East, redrawing the world map entirely.

❄️

The Cold War

The ideological division between the US and USSR led to 45 years of Cold War, a space race, proxy wars, and a world divided between capitalism and communism.

"In war, there are no unwounded soldiers." — José Narosky

May we never forget the sacrifices made, the horrors endured, and the lessons learned.
